Vikings Radio Frequently Asked Questions

What radio station is the Vikings game on?

In the Twin Cities, Vikings games air on KFAN 100.3 FM, the flagship, and on KTLK 1130 AM. Outside the metro, about 70 Vikings Radio Network stations carry every game across Minnesota, North Dakota, South Dakota, Iowa, and Wisconsin. The station depends on where you are — click "Find Nearest Stations" to see the closest one to you right now.

What is the closest Vikings radio station to me?

This finder answers that instantly: allow location access and it calculates the distance from you to the actual FCC radio tower of every Vikings Radio Network station, then lists the 15 best stations sorted by how good they should sound at your location — estimated signal strength, with FM favored over AM at similar strength for its clearer audio (or by distance if you prefer).

What is the flagship radio station for the Minnesota Vikings?

KFAN 100.3 FM in Minneapolis-St. Paul is the flagship of the Vikings Radio Network, with KTLK 1130 AM also carrying games in the Twin Cities. Both are iHeartMedia stations under a rights deal that runs through the 2031 season.

How many radio stations broadcast Vikings games?

About 70 Vikings Radio Network stations on 74 AM and FM frequencies carry Minnesota Vikings games for the 2026 season: 49 in Minnesota, 10 in North Dakota, 7 in South Dakota, 5 in Iowa, and 3 in Wisconsin. See the complete station list.

How do I listen to the Vikings game if I'm out of range?

Outside the radio network, the official Vikings app and NFL+ stream live game audio, and SiriusXM carries every NFL game on a channel that changes each week. Check the Vikings' weekly how-to-listen notes for the current channels.

How does the Vikings Radio Finder work?

It uses your device's GPS to determine your location, then calculates the distance to actual FCC radio tower locations for each station in the network. Sort by distance or by what should sound best — signal estimates use the FCC's own propagation curves with each station's licensed power and antenna height, account for AM stations' mandatory nighttime power cuts, and favor FM over AM at similar strength for its clearer audio (how it works). Not where you'll be listening? Right-click or press and hold on the map to rank stations for that spot instead. Your location stays on your device and is never sent to a server.
