2026 Vikings Radio Network Station List

Minnesota Vikings games are broadcast on about 70 Vikings Radio Network affiliate stations on 74 AM and FM frequencies across Minnesota, North Dakota, South Dakota, Iowa, and Wisconsin for the 2026 season. The flagship is KFAN 100.3 FM in the Twin Cities, with KTLK 1130 AM also carrying games.
